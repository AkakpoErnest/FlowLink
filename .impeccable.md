# FlowLink — Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
FlowLink is a B2B crypto payment platform on HashKey Chain. Two distinct audiences share the surface:

- **Businesses (primary)** — operators who invoice clients, share payment links, run payroll, and deploy AI agents for recurring transfers. They want money movement to be reliable and legible without needing MetaMask or deep crypto fluency. Their context: a work dashboard, often returning daily to check payment status and agent activity.
- **Payers (secondary)** — recipients of an invoice or payment link (`/l/[code]`). They may have **zero crypto experience**. Their context: a one-shot, branded checkout they hit from a link. The job is "pay this confidently and leave," not "learn Web3."

The job-to-be-done: move money on-chain with the ease and trust of traditional fintech, while compliance/settlement (HSP) stays invisible until it matters.

### Brand Personality
**Three words: calm, confident, approachable.**

- **Voice/tone**: Honest, specific, human. Plain language over jargon. State capabilities as truths, never aspirations. (PLAN.md sets the bar: "Stripe wouldn't write this" — generic enterprise copy was deliberately removed.)
- **Emotional goal**: The interface should make moving real money feel *calm and in control* — the product does the worrying so the user doesn't. For first-time payers, it should feel *approachable and safe*, never intimidating.
- **Not institutional-cold, not hype**: It earns trust through restraint and precision, not through compliance theater or crypto exuberance.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: **Dark-first.** The dark teal/slate palette is the canonical "FlowLink dashboard design system" and the landing experience. Light mode is supported and must not break, but dark is where polish lives.
- **Color**: Emerald/mint primary (`#34d399`, `hsl(152 69% 52%)`) on a deep slate-teal base (`#0f172a` → `#0d2d2d`). Mint is the single hero accent — used for primary actions, focus rings, and the "flow" energy, not splashed everywhere. Subtle emerald glows and a quiet particle/connection-line background reinforce "flow" without noise.
- **Type**: DM Sans for UI (light/extralight weights for display headlines — note the hero uses `font-light`/`font-extralight`). Geist Mono for numbers, amounts, addresses, codes, and anything ledger-like.
- **Surfaces**: Glassmorphic but restrained — `white/[0.08]` fills, hairline `white/15` borders, `0.75rem` radius, generous spacing, soft shadows. Motion is subtle and purposeful (staggered fade-ups, gentle floats), never bouncy or attention-grabbing.
- **Quality bar**: Stripe-grade typographic and spacing discipline; Linear/Vercel-grade dark minimalism.
- **Anti-references (explicit)**:
  - **NOT generic AI slop** — no filler copy, no aspirational badges (SOC2/ISO/"certified" it doesn't hold), no invented percentages or `$2.4M+`/`99.7%` fake stats, no decimal-precision theater (`0.3s per check`). Every number on screen must be real or clearly illustrative.
  - **NOT crypto-bro** — no hype, no neon-gradient overload, no "to the moon" energy, no lambo aesthetic. Mint-on-slate stays sophisticated, not garish.

### Design Principles
1. **Honesty over polish-that-lies.** Never display a number, badge, or claim that isn't true. If data is empty, show an honest empty state — not a fake-populated demo. This is the project's foundational rule (see PLAN.md).
2. **Calm by restraint.** One hero accent (mint), generous negative space, light type weights, quiet motion. When in doubt, remove. Confidence reads as calm, not as more.
3. **Dark-first, real money legibility.** Design and verify in dark mode first. Treat amounts, tokens, and addresses as first-class data — monospace, aligned, unambiguous. Light mode must remain functional.
4. **Two audiences, one calm system.** The business dashboard can be dense and powerful; the payer checkout must be radically simple and reassuring. Both feel like the same trustworthy product.
5. **Functional accessibility, no excuses on the basics.** No formal WCAG-level target, but never ship broken a11y: keyboard-navigable, visible focus states (the mint ring), legible contrast on the dark palette, and respect for reduced-motion on the decorative animations.
